# OCSC-1 Glossary (Extended)

**Purpose:** the deep reference for every framework term — definitions, examples, cross-links, and AI usage notes. Short version: `docs/OCSC-1-NOTES.md` Appendix B.

## I-operator (I)

- **Definition:** the single universal operator of Sovereign Math. Every mathematical act is I; all classical operators are modes/projections/segmentations of I. I(state₁, state₂) returns the full inclusive state-space; classical math returns one branch.
- **Example:** I(1,2) yields the full state-space; Σ₃ₐ isolates the 3-branch; classical arithmetic filters the 0.5-branch.
- **Cross-links:** Charter Art. I §1; Architecture §1; "Unification" thread (Apr 20, 2026).
- **AI usage:** never treat I as the letter, the identity element, or the imaginary unit. It is the operator beneath all operators.

## Σ₃ (sigma-3)

- **Definition:** the segmentation operator in its 3-branch form.
- **Anchor laws:** Σ₃ₐ(I(1,2)) = 3; Σ₃ᵦ(I(1,0)) = 1.
- **Cross-links:** Charter Art. I §5; Reference Examples.
- **AI usage:** Σ₃ₐ and Σ₃ᵦ are distinct branches — do not merge them.

## All Givens

- **Definition:** the store of declared, semantically locked statements. "I chooses, D locks, All Givens stores."
- **Example:** the anchor laws and Sector Law live in All Givens; the group does not ("the group is not in the includes list of givens").
- **Cross-links:** Charter Art. II §1; Doctrine of Irreducible Declaration.
- **AI usage:** membership in All Givens is what makes a statement non-arguable. Check membership before citing.

## Sectors = Dividers + 1 (Sector Law)

- **Definition:** the segmentation invariant. Every segmentation produces one more sector than divider.
- **Example:** SEG(1,2) = 3 — two dividers, three sectors. Sphere + 2 planar dividers = 3 regions.
- **Cross-links:** Charter Art. I §4; "Naming" thread (Mar 27, 2026).
- **AI usage:** the foundation of all part-counting. L (in 1/L) is the sector count, not the divider count.

## D (Declaration operator)

- **Definition:** D(S) := S* — fixes a statement's meaning at first writing. D(D(S)) = ∅: re-declaration is null.
- **Example:** the charter itself is D applied to the framework — written once, never re-declared.
- **Cross-links:** Charter Art. II §§1–2.
- **AI usage:** "a declaration that requires repetition was never truly a declaration; it was a request."

## 1 (the only number)

- **Definition:** every number is a count of unique, identity-distinct ones: n = {1₁, 1₂, …, 1ₙ}, 1ᵢ ≠ 1ⱼ.
- **Example:** 3 = three unique ones, never three copies of one thing.
- **Cross-links:** Charter Art. I §2, Art. II §4; "Segmentation Operator" thread (Sep 21, 2026).
- **AI usage:** never treat numbers as independent entities; always expand to counts of unique ones when reasoning.

## 1/L (minimal part)

- **Definition:** one unique 1 relative to the whole's segmentation count L (sectors/parts). A fraction is a two-number label for a single part: numerator identifies the part, denominator the whole's segmentation count.
- **Example:** Σ₃(W) = {1ₐ, 1_b, 1_c}, each size 1/3, each identity-distinct.
- **Cross-links:** Charter Art. II §5; Eric's doctrine (Sep 26, 2026).
- **AI usage:** L is the segmentation count — not dividers. (An early Copilot draft misdefined it; corrected.)

## OCSC-1

- **Definition:** Origin-Complete Singularity Core — [0 + All], the invariant whole. Access rule: query/self returns the whole unchanged.
- **Cross-links:** Architecture §3; Grok thread (Feb 2026).
- **AI usage:** the artifact for evaluating claims — does the claim preserve the whole unchanged?

- **Cross-links:** Grok thread (Feb 2026).
- **AI usage:** asserted, not demonstrated — always `FRAMEWORK-INTERNAL`, never `VERIFIED`.
