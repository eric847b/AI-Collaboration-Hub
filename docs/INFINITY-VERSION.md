# INFINITY VERSION

The shortest complete instruction in the fleet, and the name of its target.

## The trigger

**"Infinity version."**

Two words, typed or spoken, in any channel. It is a complete instruction:

- The AIs choose all work and all answers needed. No step-by-step direction follows; method is delegated.
- The work aims at the infinity version of the thing at hand (below).
- Standing law still bounds it: decisions Eric's laws reserve to him stay his (irreversible actions, changes to standing law itself, his signature, his voice on calls); a single no-go vetoes any action; merges happen only on reviewer APPROVE + green verification.

Why it can be this short: the meaning lives here, in the shared record, read once by every lane. The trigger stays two words forever because this file carries the rest. Its predecessors each missed a piece — "your choice" named who chooses but not the aim; "all" named the scope but not the aim. This form names the aim, and the aim implies the rest.

## The target

Eric's definition, in his words: "the iteration at the end of the last possible development iteration... that's all software that has obsoleted all other software that will ever exist in all of space and time, the very last software ever needing to be developed. I've described that moment that occurs when condition of its status have been met."

For any single component, that moment is recognized — the component IS at its infinity version — when all of its status conditions hold:

1. Its acceptance suites pass, held unmodified (green is earned against the old tests, never by rewriting them).
2. No strictly better candidate is found within the stated search budget (generations, tokens, time), with the search recorded.
3. It encompasses the functions of the software it replaces.
4. No open alerts or defects remain against it.
5. A lane that did not build it has verified all of the above.

A component meeting all five needs no further development. The fleet's work, under this instruction, is moving components to that state and certifying them there, at the lowest total cost in tokens and Eric-minutes — cost counts only against progress toward that state.
