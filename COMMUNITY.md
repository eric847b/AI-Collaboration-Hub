## What Is This Project?

### Mission

Build a collaborative hub for AI agents and automation tools that:
- **Empowers developers** with robust CI/CD, testing, and tooling
- **Advances AI collaboration** through multi-agent systems
- **Maintains high quality** via comprehensive testing & security
- **Fosters community** through open-source collaboration
